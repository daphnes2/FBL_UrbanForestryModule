Tree Equity Score reflects the distribution of the urban tree canopy in relation to socio-economic factors with a score out of 100. A high value reflects areas whose tree canopy is suitably beneficial, whereas a low value reflects areas where the tree canopy doesn't meet the needs of its residents.
